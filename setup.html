Open Pato

Pato: sync your bank emails with your own Claude

Pato reads your bank alert emails and logs your spending for you. You run the sync with your own Claude, so nobody else sees your emails.

First steps in the app

1. Add it to your home screen: open the app link, tap the Share button (iPhone) or the ⋮ menu (Android), then Add to Home Screen. Open it from there from now on.

2. Log what you have saved today: on Savings, under Log a transaction, pick Deposit, type the amount, choose the source Starting balance, and tap Log deposit.

3. Add loans people owe you (if any) in the Loans tab.

4. Change your color or tabs anytime from the gear at the top right.

Automatic bank sync (optional)

What you need

- A Pato account. Open https://marcoferrando114.github.io/Sette-Cifre/, tap Create an account, and finish the setup.

- A paid Claude plan (Pro or Max).

- Your bank sends you an email for every card purchase. Turn on email alerts in your bank app if they're off.

Setup (about 10 minutes, one time)

1. Connect Gmail to Claude. In claude.ai, go to Settings > Connectors and connect Gmail.

2. Create a cloud environment. Go to claude.ai/code and create a new environment (you don't need to pick a repository).

- Network access: allow these sites: firestore.googleapis.com, identitytoolkit.googleapis.com, marcoferrando114.github.io.

- Environment variables: add STAKD_EMAIL = the email you use for Pato, and STAKD_PASSWORD = your Pato password. Type them only in the environment settings, never in a chat.

3. Make the sync prompt yours. Copy the sync prompt at the bottom of this page and fill in the three blanks: your bank's name, the email address your alerts come from, and the last 4 digits of your card.

4. Schedule it. In Claude Code, using that environment, create a scheduled task that runs the prompt twice a day (for example 8:00 and 14:00 Santo Domingo time), with the Gmail connector turned on.

5. Test it once. Run the task now. Then open Pato and look at Spend: your recent purchases should be there.

Good to know

- The sync only writes into your own account. It can't see anyone else's data, and nobody else can see yours.

- If your bank changes its email format, ask Claude to adjust the prompt.

- To stop syncing, turn off or delete the scheduled task.

Sync prompt

Fill in the three <blanks> before using it.

Sync my bank alert emails from Gmail into my Pato app. Work quietly; this is routine.

SETUP (every run): download the helper and use it for all reads and writes. It signs in as me with the environment variables STAKD_EMAIL and STAKD_PASSWORD. Never print, store or ask for the password.
  curl -sSfo /tmp/stakd_db.py https://marcoferrando114.github.io/Sette-Cifre/tools/stakd_db.py
  python3 -I /tmp/stakd_db.py list spend
  python3 -I /tmp/stakd_db.py get spendMeta sync
  python3 -I /tmp/stakd_db.py set spend <GMAIL_MESSAGE_ID> '<json>'
  python3 -I /tmp/stakd_db.py update spendMeta sync '<json>'
If sign-in fails, stop and tell me in one line.

MY BANK: <BANK NAME>. Alerts come from <SENDER ADDRESS, e.g. alertas@mybank.com>. My card ends in <LAST 4>.

1. List the "spend" collection; every doc id there is a Gmail message id already synced.
2. Search Gmail: from:<SENDER ADDRESS> newer_than:10d. Open every thread (get_thread) and handle each message whose id is not synced yet. Read it as plain text.
3. For each card purchase or cash withdrawal, create one spend doc with doc id = the Gmail message id:
   {date:"YYYY-MM-DD" (transaction date in the email), merchant: readable title-case name, raw: merchant text as in the email, amount: number in the original currency, currency:"DOP"|"USD"|"EUR", rd: amount in RD$ (USD×60, EUR×73, DOP×1), kind:"consumo" (or "retiro" for cash withdrawals, cat "cash"), status:"Aprobada" or "Declinada", cat, source:"email", excluded:false, ts: the email's internalDate in ms}
   Categories: food (restaurants, cafés, fast food), delivery (Uber Eats, PedidosYa), groceries (supermarkets), gas (gas stations, parking, tolls, car wash, car repairs), sport (gyms, clubs, sports), out (bars, trips, hotels), shopping (clothes, electronics, games), subs (subscriptions), rides (Uber rides, taxis), health (pharmacies), family, other (unclear).
   Skip verification codes, statements and marketing. Transfers I send: kind "transfer", merchant "Transfer to <name>", cat "other".
4. Update spendMeta/sync with {lastSync: today YYYY-MM-DD, lastRunAt: now in ms}.
5. Only tell me about declined purchases or a single purchase over RD$10,000. Otherwise just say how many were added.
